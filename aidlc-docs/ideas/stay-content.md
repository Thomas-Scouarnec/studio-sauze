## Decided outline (2026-09-22)

### 1. Welcome message
- Two or three warm lines, signed, with how to reach the owners during the stay

### 2. Before the arrival
- **What to bring:** bed linen and towels are **not provided**; guests bring their own
- **Arrival from 4 pm**, departure **before 11 am**
- **Key handover:** a local person hands over the keys at arrival time
- **Winter driving:** loi Montagne reminder — winter tyres, or chains/socks carried in the car, compulsory from 1 November to 31 March
- **Groceries:** recommend doing the shopping at the **Intermarché in Barcelonnette** on the way up, with a Google Maps link

### 3. Arrival
- Address and precise access to the public parking (you need to go up; a residence with a similar name is next to it)
- Other parking places in case there is no space
- How to get to the flat: lift to the **1st floor**, flat **no. 10**, on the **left** coming out of the lift
- No Wi-Fi, but good 4G/5G coverage (already stated on the public site)

### 4. Flat information
- Inventory of the main items
- How to use the hot plates / oven
- How to open the bed
- How to use the washing machine
- Ski locker: where it is, and how to open it (same key as the main door)
- Pillows and blankets (provided; linen is not)
- **Kitchen basics provided:** salt, oil, coffee, dishwasher tablets, cleaning products
- **Something broken?** Contact us by phone or email

### 5. Activities — split winter / summer
- Hikes, trails; snowshoeing (raquettes) in winter
- Activities with kids
- Rainy days: **board games** available in the flat
- Restaurants: a few favourites

### 6. Shops and services
- Supermarket, boulangerie, pharmacy, doctor, nearest hospital

### 7. Practical
- Where to take the rubbish
- **Residence rules:** no skis upstairs; no walking around the residence in ski boots

### 8. Before leaving
- Short checkout checklist

### 9. After leaving
- Thanks for the stay; feedback welcome at the contact email; a word-of-mouth recommendation is welcome
